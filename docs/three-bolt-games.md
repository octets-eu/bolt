# Games for three Bolts

Ideas for games once a third Bolt joins. None of them is built. Each is described with the senses from `hide-and-seek.md`: a Bolt calls, hears a call and its channel, and feels a bump; the referee is connected to all three. Each idea lists what it needs beyond a working game of hide and seek.

## What a third Bolt changes

- **Channels tell callers apart.** A message carries its channel, so two Bolts can call at once and a listener knows which one it hears.
- **A bump no longer names the other Bolt.** With two Bolts a bump felt by both is the tag. With three, the referee sees which two reported a bump within a moment of each other, and that pair is the bump.
- **Roles can be shared or opposed.** Two Bolts can want the same thing, or each can want something different from the other two.

## The games

Ordered from the smallest step beyond hide and seek to the largest.

### Two seekers

One hider calls, two seekers look for it at the same time. A bump between the two seekers is not a tag; both back off and go on. There are two ways to score it:

- **As a race:** the first seeker whose bump the hider also reports wins.
- **As a team:** the seekers play together against the clock, and the game ends when either of them tags the hider.

- Needs: a seeker that treats a bump the hider did not report as an obstacle, which is the same sense as going round a table leg.

### Two hiders

Two hiders call on different channels, one seeker finds both. The seeker chooses which call to follow, tags that hider, then turns to the other. The game ends at the second tag.

- Needs: two ladders in the air at once, and a seeker that listens to one channel and ignores the other.
- Open: whether two ladders sent at the same time disturb each other at the receiver.

### Relay

A seeks B. At the tag B becomes the seeker and C starts to call; at the next tag C seeks A. The round ends when every Bolt has been tagged once.

- Needs: a Bolt that switches between calling and seeking on the referee's word. This is the "second game with the roles swapped" from `hide-and-seek.md`, done twice.

### Guard and thief

One Bolt is the treasure: it stands still and calls. The thief seeks it as in hide and seek. The guard hears the same call and stays near the treasure, moving about in front of it. A bump between thief and treasure is a win for the thief; a bump between thief and guard is a win for the guard.

- Needs: a guard that holds a distance to the call instead of driving into it, using how loud the call arrives.
- Open: whether loudness is steady enough to hold a distance by.

### Two hunters, one prey

The prey calls and moves. Two hunters seek it. The game ends when one hunter tags the prey. Two hunters that arrive from different sides leave the prey less room than two that follow the same path.

- Needs: a hider that moves, which `hide-and-seek.md` lists as not in the game yet.
- Needs for cooperation: the hunters know of each other, either by a call of their own on a third channel or through the referee. If the referee passes anything on, it must not be the prey's position.

### Circle chase

A hunts B, B hunts C, C hunts A. Every Bolt calls on its own channel and listens only for the channel of its prey. Every Bolt is hunter and prey at once. The first tag ends the game: the tagged Bolt is out and its hunter wins, or the game goes on as hide and seek between the two that remain.

- Needs: a Bolt that calls and seeks at the same time, and a hider that moves.
- Open: whether a Bolt hears another's call while it is sending its own.

### Piggy in the middle

Two Bolts stand apart and call to each other in turn; each call that arrives counts as a catch. The third Bolt tries to get between them so that the call does not arrive. After a set number of missed calls the middle Bolt swaps with the caller that missed.

- Open: whether a Bolt standing between two others stops the call. The game exists only if it does.

## Order of work

Two seekers, two hiders and relay need no moving hider and no new sense beyond those already planned for hide and seek. Guard and thief adds holding a distance. Two hunters and circle chase wait for the hider that moves. Piggy in the middle waits for one experiment.
