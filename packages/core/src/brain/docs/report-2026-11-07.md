## What changed and why

1. **The camera's landmarks** (`utils/rig.mjs`). A lamp now lights the floor. The left partition part's top is darker than the boards, so the detector found a board and every locate threw "landmarks implausible". The mount has not moved: the right end detects at the same pixel as on 2026-11-06. `locate` now takes a detected end only within 15 px of last session's, else the known pixel.
2. **Why the corner reads hardly landed** (2 to 3 % of volleys in the last session). The cause was measured, not guessed:
   - `utils/link-load.mjs` showed that link load is not the cause. Standing, with one channel at a fixed strength, the volleys land in 30 to 36 % of beats, with or without the brain's other traffic. With either ladder they land in 3 to 8 %.
   - `utils/jitter.mjs` showed that under the ladder the notifies scatter ±40 ms about one beat, against ±15 ms with a fixed channel.
   - `utils/frame-timing.mjs`, which times frames against the program's own send commands, showed why. Within a round, each level's frame comes about 25 ms later after its command than the one before, and the first of the next round comes early again. The delay belongs to the position in the round, not to the channel: channel 3 comes at 107 ms when it leads the round and at 185 ms when it closes it. One beat for all levels smeared the volley over 50 to 75 ms.
3. **The fix in the observation layer**: each channel keeps its own expected notify, recurring once a round and pulled 40 % toward each notify that comes within 40 ms of it. A volley goes before the soonest expected frame of a channel heard in the last 3 s, so there are no reads for levels that do not arrive. The sender's commands now run on an absolute 200 ms schedule (`utils/ladder.mjs`, the library's ladders sent through the infrared actuator), so the round is the program's. Named reads went from about 7 per run to 20 to 40.
4. **Stand to hear** (`motor.mjs`). Turning or driving past the sender, reads hardly land; standing and facing it, they land in most volleys. So:
   - The scan turns in 45° steps and stands 1.3 s after each, as the sweep listens a round per heading.
   - On the axis (`M_AXIS`), the seeker stands and listens 2.5 s for a corner read to name the side, and only then probes ahead. This replaces the old probe that started at once on a coin toss.
5. **`IR_MISS` is gated by an efference copy, `self.still`** (under 20° turned in the last second; `sensory_map.jsonc`, `brain.mjs`). Turning, the lobe leaves the sender within the round. The miss then erased a bump of four corner reads that the seeker was turning toward (run 10:38, 4.1 to 5.2 s).
6. **Near, on the short ladder (`IR_NEAR`)**, the weave narrows from ±45° to ±15°, and a sender more than 30° off is turned to in place. With the full weave and arcs, run 10:28 circled the sender at 13 to 20 cm for 30 s.
7. **The stop**: the motor goes off first, then the subscriptions. Unsubscribing first, the last roll carried the seeker 40 cm past the tag (10:15).
8. **Network steps of at least 25 ms** (`brain.mjs`). Motion samples are timed by their arrival, and a bunched pair would have taken an event in at a tiny weight. This was rare (5 to 16 per run), so it is hygiene, not a cause.

The connectome is unchanged in `circuit.json` (34 neurons, 400 edges). The sensory map's `IR_MISS` channel gained `mul self.still`, and `beatMs` is now the sender's 200 ms schedule.
