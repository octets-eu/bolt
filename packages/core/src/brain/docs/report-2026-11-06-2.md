## What changed and why (in the order the floor taught it)

1. **The camera tracks the seeker during a run** (`utils/track.mjs`). The seeker is lit white; each fresh frame is compared with the start's dark frame, with the sender's spot masked out (it shows the ladder on its matrix). The plan point goes to the trace as `cam <s> (x, y) to sender <cm>`. The stream lags 2 to 4 s, so the times are about right and the fence is late. This is what made the failures readable.
2. **What the track showed**: approaches that run straight but 15 to 20 cm to one side of the sender, then pass it at 12 to 23 cm and drive on. The cause is in the observation: a carrier frame feeds the ring at nose and tail, and the lobe is wide, so any heading inside it confirms itself. Fixes:
   - **Casting** in target mode (`motor.mjs`): the nose weaves ±45° over 2.5 s, past the lobe's edge, so frames heard at one end of the swing pile up on that side (the ring is yaw-shifted).
   - **`IR_MISS`**: a whole ladder round heard with stronger levels but no carrier inhibits the ring at nose and tail (cos⁴, -0.6). This is the negative evidence that was missing.
   - **The closeness gradient only weighs carrier frames** (`mul frame.carrier` in the map). Fed on its own, it pinned the ring to wherever the nose pointed.
   - **The carrier-step channels are removed.** The carrier stepping down is as often the nose turning into the lobe as the sender coming closer; it tipped a run onto the wrong end of the axis at 3 s.
   - **No false "approach" at start-up**: the closeness has no mean yet, so the start counts as a ladder switch (`switchedAt`).
3. **Slower**: `seekSpeed` went 38 → 26 → 32 (about 17 cm/s). At 21 cm/s a cast covered 80 cm and runs passed by; at 13 cm/s contacts were too soft to report.
4. **`AXIAL` 1.0 → 1.5**: the first bump forms in one pass of the lobe. Before, it took about 10 s of scanning before the first move.
5. **Collision thresholds** (`seek.mjs`): no speed terms for either Bolt. With the firmware's default speed term, a seeker pressed against the sender for 20 s and reported nothing. The seeker is at 15, the standing sender at 10. The seeker now fires on its own driving about once a second (y 15 to 21); that is harmless, because the standing sender fires only on contact and the tag needs both within 300 ms. A rolling sender (`--move`) keeps the speed term at 20.
6. **Volley**: 3 reads 7 ms apart from a 60 ms lead (`sensory_map.jsonc`). 5 reads landed hardly more often, and seemed to load the link.
7. **Safety**:
   - **The fence**: no-go boxes with margin for the cables, the cabinet, the chest, the right partition's end, furniture-2 and the walls, in `seek.mjs`.
   - **The runaway guard** is now locator start + 100 cm.
   - **A watchdog**: no brain step for 1.5 s ends the run as `stalled`.
   - **The stop**: each step capped at 3 s, the seeker stopped first.

## Open problems, measured

- **A contact without a tag is still common.** In 6 runs the sender felt a contact (x 10 to 26) that the seeker reported 0.4 to 1.5 s later or not at all, while the camera showed them touching. The seeker's link carries reads, rolls, listen re-arms, the matrix and the motion stream; its notifications may arrive late. The collision notification's firmware timestamp reads **0**, so it cannot correct this. I tried a clock-based tag on it (18:41) and it was broken: the run is counted not found, though by arrival times both reported at 17.5 s. That code is removed.
- **The first lock is still a coin toss between nose and tail** when no corner read lands. Run 18:12 drove 30 cm the wrong way into the fence. Scaling speed by the ring's length (tried at 18:15) made the start dither for 20 s and was reverted.
- **Carrier 1 (strength 1, short ladder) does not mean near**: it was heard 1 m past the sender in the tail lobe. The ladder gives no reliable distance under about 1 m.
- **The seeker's motion stream once stopped mid-run** (18:25), and the seeker rolled on into the cabinet on its last command. place.mjs reached it afterwards. The watchdog now catches this.
- The matrix view's nose orientation is still not checked by camera.
