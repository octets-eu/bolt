Session budget $10. About $5.3 used when this was written. The session ended on a Bolt the camera cannot see: SB-9129 is hidden near the chest (questions.md, last section). Both Bolts are asleep, the camera is off.

## What exists now

The seeker's brain, the connectome as data plus the code that runs it:

- `connectome/circuit.json`, 33 neurons and 386 edges in the worm's format, written by `connectome/build-circuit.mjs`, which holds every wiring constant. Rebuild after a change: `npx tsx packages/core/src/brain/connectome/build-circuit.mjs`.
  - Observation: `IR_FL IR_FR IR_BR IR_BL` (corner receivers), `IR_AX` (carrier frame, nose or tail), `IR_APPROACH IR_RECEDE` (gradient), `IR_ANY` (presence), `IR_NEAR` (ladder).
  - Ring: `R00`..`R15`, egocentric, 0° = nose, clockwise. Leaky (250 ms), clamped 0..1, weights a1 cos d + a2 cos 2d − b (0.24, 0.12, 0.03), tuned in simulation. One corner read is held about 5 s and a full bump fades over about 10 s. A nose-and-tail pair has no vector and lasts only while fed. The ring is shifted against the Bolt's own turn, taken from the yaw (`ring/ring.mjs`).
  - Motor: `M_FWD M_BACK M_RIGHT M_LEFT` read the ring's vector. `M_SEARCH` (bias 1) is on unless that vector has a length. `M_AXIS` (cos 2θ) is on when the ring holds an axis along the nose. `M_ROAM` is on when nothing is heard. `REQ_SHORT` asks for the short ladder.
- `connectome/sensory_map.jsonc`: the IR facts the observation layer uses (channels, corners, beat, volley timing, carrier and near windows), and the channels source → ops → neuron. The ops are gain, sub, mul, clamp, rate and adapt (the worm's dC/dt).
- `connectome/observation/observation.mjs`: listen notifies (re-armed each time) → levels, carrier, near/short-ladder hysteresis, closeness. It learns the beat from the last 10 notifies and fires a volley of 2 corner reads per beat, past the queue.
- `connectome/network.mjs` runs the data. `connectome/motor/motor.mjs` turns rates into a roll, with four modes: target (turn to the ring's direction, drive in proportion to how far ahead it lies), probe (axis known, side not: roll ahead slowly, the gradient decides), scan (turn in place at 70°/s), and roam. `connectome/brain.mjs` ties it to a Bolt: one step per motion sample, and one roll per beat, sent after the reads' replies.
- `utils/matrix-view.mjs`: the ring live on the seeker's matrix. The border pixel toward the ring's direction is green (target), cyan (probe), blue (scan) or dim red (roam). Not checked by camera which matrix edge is the nose.
- `seek.mjs`, the entry point: camera start distance → ladder on the sender, brain on the seeker → tag (both collisions at threshold 20, within 300 ms) / time (1.5 × goal) / runaway (locator > start + 50 cm) → camera end distance → ledger row. Trace per second to `frames/<stamp>-trace.txt`. `--move` makes the sender roll a slow square; it is written but never run.
- `place.mjs`: drives a Bolt to a plan spot by camera in time-capped legs, learning phi from the first leg. Its 4th argument is the first leg's heading (180 backs away from a contact). Speed 40 gives 22 to 27 cm/s.
- Rig helpers in `utils/`: `rig.mjs` (bring up with one retry, locate, leg, letGo), `read-timing.mjs` (calibration of the read lead), `look.mjs` and `faint.mjs` (finding a Bolt by eye or by faint light).

## What the floor taught

- **Corner reads are the only side cue, and they are scarce.** In a quiet calibration (`utils/read-timing.mjs`, SB-11DF sending one channel 62 to 130 cm away), one read lands in about 25 % of frames at a 45 to 55 ms lead, none at 20 or 60. Turning in place doesn't hurt (4 of 15). Strength 16 floods all four corners at 130 cm, so it carries no side. Strength 4 at 62 cm lands about 10 % and names one corner. During seeks only 0 to 3 % landed. A roll written between the fired reads and their replies seemed to cost them; it now goes out after the replies. The last run (9) still landed 5 of 316, so this is **not solved**.
- **The gradient works for distance, not for side.** The closeness rate (log2(32/strength) per frame heard), against its own 4 s mean, rises clearly while driving at the sender. With it, run 8 locked a direction in 4 s and held it. But nose and tail, and left and right, come only from corner reads. Without reads, a drive that starts a few degrees off stays off and passes the sender by 1 to 3 cm.
- Strength 2 (long ladder) is heard at 1 m, so the brain asks for the short ladder within seconds of the start. The short ladder's 1 marks the last 30 cm or so.
- After a sleep, the next connect sometimes fails in takeover (badDeviceId, no ack). `bringUp` now retries once after 3 s. The camera's `frame` sometimes gave no fresh frame for 15 s on the first try in a process, then worked on the next.
