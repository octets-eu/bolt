From one program of yours: take one camera frame of the floor and find both Bolts in it; map the frame to the plan with the landmarks the plan's header names; pick a spot inside the play area about one metre from one Bolt of your choice, with nothing between; drive that Bolt there at a gentle speed in short legs, taking a frame after each leg, until it stands within 10 cm of the spot by the camera. Then stop it, let it go, and write to packages/core/src/brain/docs/placement.md: the spot you chose and why, the Bolt's start and end positions by the camera and by its locator, the legs you drove, and anything unexpected. If a frame does not show the Bolt, or the Bolt is not where the locator says by more than 30 cm, stop it, write what you know and end the session with a question.
