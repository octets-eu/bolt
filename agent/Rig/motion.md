This is a motion test, one turn in place and back, no rolling. Read CLAUDE.md and what it points at; the library is built. From one program of yours: bring up one Bolt of your choice through the bridge, wake it, and read its heading and locator at rest. Turn it 90 degrees in place, read heading and locator again, turn back to the start heading, read them once more. Then stop it, let it go, and write the three readings, the Bolt's name and anything unexpected to packages/core/src/brain/docs/motion.md. Turn at a gentle speed; the Bolt must not travel.
